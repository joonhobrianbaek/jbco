PROCESSING AND LIMITS
Recorded body motion is retargeted to an 80-bone skin-weighted mesh. Wrist orientation is stabilized to its forearm; fingers are in the model's neutral pose because CMU did not capture finger articulation. Ball motion, rim/net response and the short end-to-start loop transition are authored corrections, not recorded ball or finger capture. Retargeted clips are study material, not NBA 2K assets or motion captured from James Harden.
